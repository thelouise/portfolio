DNAme
Developed a lightweight DNA sequence analysis tool to identify genetic patterns or motifs. Supported input of FASTA-format DNA sequences and performed basic matching, GC content calculation, and sequence cleaning. Could be extended to detect mutations, generate reverse complements, or simulate transcription.
